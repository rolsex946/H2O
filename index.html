About Us

The H2O FITNESS Story

A decade of transforming lives through fitness, discipline, and community.

Our Mission

Founded in 2012 by former national athlete Arjun Mehra, H2O FITNESS Singur was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to world-class fitness facilities and guidance. What started as a 2,000 sq. ft. garage gym has evolved into a 20,000 sq. ft. premium fitness destination serving over 5,000 active members.

We don't just build bodies — we build confidence, discipline, and a community that uplifts each other. Our approach combines cutting-edge sports science with personalized attention, ensuring every member gets the roadmap they need to achieve their goals.
